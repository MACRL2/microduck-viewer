🐤 microduck-viewer

Interactive, in-browser simulations of Pollen Robotics' microduck — a small articulated balancing robot. MuJoCo (WebAssembly) steps the physics, a trained RL policy runs in plain JavaScript at 50 Hz, three.js renders. No server, no build.

Embeddable simulation routes:

balance ↯ walk →

Each route mounts full-viewport and is designed to be embedded via <iframe> in the MACRL2 course. It accepts ?theme=light and postMessage (microduck:push / microduck:reset / microduck:theme).

Robot assets and policies are from pollen-robotics/microduck and microduck_rl (Apache-2.0). See NOTICE.
